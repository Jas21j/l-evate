# Examine: check before you render

Render stills at 0%, 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%, plus the frame where each state has settled. Check each frame in every format.

1. **Clipped letters**: line height 1.1 or more. Check descenders (g, j, p, q, y) and the mask padding.
2. **Orphans**: no single word alone on a line.
3. **Empty stage**: the subject fills most of the frame. Scale up before adding decoration.
4. **Flat colour**: use a vignette or light fall-off, never one flat fill.
5. **Linear motion**: everything eases or springs.
6. **Loop seam**: for loops, the last frame equals the first.
7. **Text held too short**: 1.2s minimum, 1.4s for headlines.
8. **Fake logos**: use the real file.
9. **Busy background**: one idea on a quiet ground.
10. **No texture**: grain on, regenerated per frame from a seed.

L-evate additions:
- **Truth**: every number was measured, and every UI string exists in the product's source.
- **Ghosts**: elements from other scenes are hidden (look at the corners of frame 0).
- **Sound**: every visible change has a cue. The mix is at −16 LUFS with peaks ≤ −1 dBFS. Look at the waveform image.
